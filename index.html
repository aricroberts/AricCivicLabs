Why we exist

Everyone deserves a voice
backed by evidence.

"Many of us struggle to communicate the value we bring to different arenas of life. We exist to give you that voice."

There's a vicious cycle that quietly talented people know too well: you create real impact, but say nothing about it. Nobody notices. Your career stagnates. You lose opportunities to people who are louder, not better.

Aric Civic Labs builds tools to break that cycle. We translate your work into honest dollar figures, compelling narratives, and documents that open doors — whether you're negotiating a raise, applying for a grant, defending your team's headcount, or deciding between a human hire and an AI tool.

We primarily serve people who are employed, employ others, run any organization, or are part of a team. In other words: almost everyone.

How we work

Simple rules we
never break.

01 — Free
No paywalls. Ever.
The people who need these tools most often can't afford another subscription. Our core tools will always be free.
02 — Private
Your data is yours.
No accounts, no tracking, no databases. Everything lives in your browser. Destroy it in one click.
03 — Honest
Defensible, not inflated.
We use confidence weighting and attribution percentages. The numbers we help you build will hold up in any room.
04 — Civic
Built for everyone.
The word "civic" is in our name on purpose. We build for communities, not just customers.
